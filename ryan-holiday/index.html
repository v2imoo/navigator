Part I — The story

The story

Ryan Holiday became one of the most widely-read modern authors on practical philosophy by translating ancient Stoicism — the wisdom of Marcus Aurelius, Seneca and Epictetus — into direct, actionable guidance for founders, athletes, and anyone facing pressure and adversity. Books like The Obstacle Is the Way and Ego Is the Enemy made a 2,000-year-old philosophy feel urgently useful.

Before writing, Holiday was a marketing prodigy — a director of marketing in his early twenties and author of a book on media manipulation — giving him a sharp understanding of attention and persuasion. His core Stoic messages resonate deeply in business: focus only on what you can control, treat obstacles as opportunities, and guard against the ego that undoes success.

Holiday shows the enduring value of old wisdom applied to new problems. His distillation of Stoicism — control what you can, welcome obstacles, master your ego — gives decision-makers a durable operating philosophy for pressure, failure and success alike.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Focus on what you control

Holiday's central Stoic lesson: spend energy only on what's in your power.

Do this: Direct your effort at what you can control; release what you can't.
Principle 2

Turn obstacles into the way

He teaches that impediments can become the path forward.

Do this: Treat each obstacle as information and opportunity, not just a barrier.
Principle 3

Guard against ego

Ego Is the Enemy warns that ego undoes success.

Do this: Watch your ego, especially after success — it's what quietly undoes people.

In their words

The obstacle is the way.
Ego is the enemy.
You could leave life right now. Let that determine what you do and say and think.
