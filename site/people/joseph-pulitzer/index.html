Joseph Pulitzer is one of the FOUNDER profiles in Navigator's library of people and companies worth studying. This profile is being expanded into the full narrative — the origins, the turning points, the pivotal decisions, and the lessons that transfer to your own work.

The playbook — what to study

  • The early bet. What non-obvious wager did they make before it was obvious — and what did they see that others missed?
  • What they refused to do. The disciplined nos often matter more than the yeses. Where did they stay narrow?
  • The compounding advantage. What did they build that got stronger over time — a brand, a network, a system, a reputation?
  • Decisions under uncertainty. How did they act when the facts were incomplete and the stakes were high?
