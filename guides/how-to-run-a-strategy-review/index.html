How to Run a Strategy Review

A structured way to assess where you are and decide where to go.

Contents

Overview

A strategy review that's just a status update wastes everyone's time. A good one honestly assesses your position and produces clear decisions about direction.

This guide sequences the classic frameworks into a review that actually decides something.

The steps

Step 1

Take stock of your position

Start with a SWOT analysis to get an honest one-page picture of where you stand.

Step 2

Scan the environment

Run a PESTEL analysis for macro shifts and Porter&#x27;s Five Forces for industry pressures.

Step 3

Review the portfolio

Use the BCG matrix to decide what to fund, milk, fix or drop.

Step 4

Choose a growth path

Pick a direction with the Ansoff matrix, matching risk to your appetite, or look for uncontested space with Blue Ocean strategy.

Step 5

Set measurable goals

Translate the strategy into a few OKRs so it becomes action, not a slide deck.

Step 6

Decide, don't just discuss

Close with clear decisions — separate the modes of debate first with Six Thinking Hats so the review reaches conclusions.

Key takeaways

  • Assess honestly before you decide — SWOT then environment scan.
  • Make portfolio and growth choices explicit.
  • Turn strategy into measurable OKRs or it won't happen.
  • A review must produce decisions, not just discussion.

Frequently asked questions

How often should we review strategy?

Deeply once or twice a year, with lighter check-ins each quarter against your OKRs — often enough to adapt, not so often it becomes churn.

How do we avoid a review that just talks?

End every review with explicit decisions and owners. Use a structure like Six Thinking Hats to move from discussion to conclusion.
