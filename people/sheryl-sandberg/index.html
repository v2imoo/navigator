Sheryl Sandberg

The operator who built the advertising machine that turned Facebook's reach into one of history's great businesses.

4 min read · 2001–2022

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Sheryl Sandberg was the operator who turned Facebook's enormous audience into an equally enormous business. Recruited by Mark Zuckerberg in 2008 to complement his product focus, she built the advertising engine — the targeting, sales organisation and monetisation model — that made Facebook one of the most profitable companies ever, a classic pairing of visionary founder and world-class operating executive.

Earlier, at Google, she had helped scale its advertising business too, giving her a rare mastery of turning internet attention into revenue. Beyond operations, her book Lean In sparked a global conversation about women and leadership, making her one of the most influential business voices of her generation. She left Meta in 2022.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Complement the founder

Sandberg supplied the operational and business rigour to Zuckerberg's product vision.

Do this: Pair a product visionary with an operator who builds the business around it.
Principle 2

Turn attention into revenue

She built the advertising engine that monetised Facebook's reach.

Do this: Find the model that converts your core asset — here, attention — into revenue.
Principle 3

Build the organisation

Sandberg scaled the sales and operating machine, not just the strategy.

Do this: Invest in the organisation and systems that let a strategy actually scale.
