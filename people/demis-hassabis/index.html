Demis Hassabis

A chess prodigy and game designer who built the AI that cracked protein folding — and won a Nobel for it.

Part I — The story

The story

Demis Hassabis was a chess prodigy and successful video-game designer before earning a PhD in neuroscience and co-founding DeepMind in 2010 with the audacious mission to 'solve intelligence, and then use it to solve everything else'. DeepMind, acquired by Google in 2014, produced landmark systems: AlphaGo, which beat the world's best Go players; and AlphaFold, which solved the decades-old problem of predicting protein structures — a breakthrough so significant it earned Hassabis a share of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

Hassabis combines deep technical ambition with a focus on science as AI's highest purpose, now leading Google DeepMind at the frontier of the field. He is a study in the power of combining disparate disciplines — games, neuroscience, computation — in aiming artificial intelligence at humanity's hardest scientific problems, and in pursuing a mission vast enough to organise a career.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Combine distant disciplines

Hassabis fused games, neuroscience and computing.

Do this: Breakthroughs often come from the collision of fields; build an unusual combination of expertise others lack.
Principle 2

Aim AI at the hardest science

He pointed DeepMind at protein folding, not just games.

Do this: Direct your most powerful tools at the deepest, most consequential problems — the ones that unlock whole fields.
Principle 3

Set a mission big enough to organise everything

'Solve intelligence' gave DeepMind a north star.

Do this: Choose a mission vast and clear enough that it shapes every hire, project and decision beneath it.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Demis Hassabis?

A chess prodigy and game designer turned neuroscientist who co-founded DeepMind in 2010; its AlphaGo and AlphaFold systems were landmark AI breakthroughs, and AlphaFold earned him a share of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

What is AlphaFold?

DeepMind's AI system that solved the decades-old problem of predicting the 3-D structure of proteins from their sequence — a breakthrough for biology and medicine, central to Hassabis's Nobel Prize.
