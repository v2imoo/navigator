Danny Meyer

The restaurateur who turned hospitality into a system — and a burger stand into a global chain.

4 min read · 1985–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Danny Meyer built a restaurant empire and, more influentially, articulated a philosophy of 'enlightened hospitality' that reshaped how the industry thinks about service. His core idea: put employees first, because the way you treat your staff determines how they treat customers — hospitality flows from the inside out.

Starting with Union Square Cafe in 1985, Meyer built a collection of acclaimed New York restaurants, then turned a humble hot-dog cart in a park into Shake Shack, a global fast-casual chain — proving his hospitality principles could scale from fine dining to a burger window. He codified his approach in Setting the Table.

Meyer's contribution is the insight that hospitality — making people feel genuinely cared for — is a repeatable, teachable system and a real competitive advantage, whether in a Michelin-level restaurant or a fast-food line.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Put employees first

Meyer argued that staff, treated well, create the customer experience.

Do this: Prioritise how you treat staff — it becomes how they treat customers.
Principle 2

Systematise hospitality

He made warmth and care teachable and repeatable, not accidental.

Do this: Turn great service into an explicit, trainable system rather than luck.
Principle 3

Scale the principle, not just the product

Enlightened hospitality worked from fine dining to Shake Shack.

Do this: Identify the principle behind your success and scale that, not only the format.

In their words

Hospitality is the foundation of my business.
Business, like life, is all about how you make people feel.
Set the table so others can succeed.

Frequently asked questions

What is 'enlightened hospitality'?

Danny Meyer's philosophy that you put employees first, because how you treat staff determines how they treat customers — hospitality flows from the inside out.

How did Shake Shack start?

As a humble hot-dog cart in New York's Madison Square Park in 2004, which Meyer grew into a global fast-casual chain using the same hospitality principles as his fine-dining restaurants.
