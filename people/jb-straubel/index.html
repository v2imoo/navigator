JB Straubel

The engineering brain behind Tesla's batteries, now closing the loop by recycling them at scale.

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

JB Straubel was the technical co-founder and long-time chief technology officer of Tesla — the engineering mind behind its battery technology, powertrains and energy strategy, arguably as essential to Tesla's success as any of its better-known names. After nearly 15 years, he left to found Redwood Materials, a company tackling the next problem the EV revolution creates: recycling lithium-ion batteries and building a domestic supply chain for battery materials, closing the loop on clean energy.

Straubel saw that electrifying transport creates a massive future stream of batteries to recover and reuse. He is a study in the indispensable deep-technical co-founder, in anticipating the second-order problem your own success will create, and in building the unglamorous infrastructure that a transition actually requires.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Own the deep technical core

Straubel was Tesla's engineering brain.

Do this: Mastering the hardest technical core of a venture makes you indispensable, whatever the spotlight says.
Principle 2

Anticipate your success's side effects

He saw the coming flood of used batteries.

Do this: Every transition creates second-order problems; spot the one your own success will cause and build for it early.
Principle 3

Build the unglamorous infrastructure

Recycling isn't flashy, but it's essential.

Do this: The boring, essential infrastructure a revolution needs is often the most durable opportunity; build what must exist.
