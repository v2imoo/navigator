N. R. Narayana Murthy

The founder who built India's IT industry on a radical idea for its time: wealth created legally, ethically, and shared.

4 min read · 1981–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Narayana Murthy co-founded Infosys in 1981 with six colleagues and a tiny borrowed sum, and built it into a global IT-services giant that, alongside a few peers, created India's modern technology industry and put the country on the world's economic map. Infosys pioneered the 'global delivery model' — doing high-quality software work from India for clients worldwide — proving Indian talent could compete at the highest level and building an industry that employs millions.

Murthy's deeper contribution was ethical. In an era when Indian business was often associated with cutting corners, he insisted Infosys create wealth legally and ethically, with world-class corporate governance and transparency, and famously shared equity widely with employees, creating thousands of 'dollar millionaires'. 'The softest pillow is a clear conscience,' he said. He is a study in building an industry from nothing, in competing globally on talent and quality, and in the long-term value of uncompromising integrity and shared ownership.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Compete globally on talent and quality

Infosys proved Indian talent could deliver world-class work from India.

Do this: Compete on the quality of your talent, wherever it sits, not on proximity or cost alone.
Principle 2

Make integrity a competitive edge

Murthy built wealth legally and ethically when that was rare.

Do this: Uncompromising integrity and governance build trust that compounds over decades.
Principle 3

Share the ownership

He spread equity widely, creating thousands of employee millionaires.

Do this: Share ownership broadly; aligned, invested people build a stronger company.

In their words

The softest pillow is a clear conscience.
Love your job but never fall in love with your company.
Performance leads to recognition; recognition brings respect; respect enhances power.
