Steven Spielberg

The most commercially successful director in history — who understood the business of storytelling as well as the craft.

4 min read · 1971–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Steven Spielberg is the most commercially successful film director in history, defining the modern blockbuster with Jaws and going on to make some of the most beloved and profitable films ever. But he is also a shrewd businessman who understood that the biggest rewards come from ownership and control, not just craft — negotiating profit participation and, eventually, building his own studio.

In 1994 he co-founded DreamWorks SKG with David Geffen and Jeffrey Katzenberg, an ambitious bid to build a new major studio owned by its creators. Spielberg mastered the commercial instincts of what audiences wanted while maintaining artistic ambition, and consistently structured deals to share in the upside of the hits he created rather than work purely for a fee.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Master craft and commerce

Spielberg combined artistic ambition with commercial instinct.

Do this: Excel at the craft, but understand the business that turns it into value.
Principle 2

Structure for the upside

He took profit participation, sharing in his hits' success.

Do this: Structure deals so you share in the upside of what you create, not just a fee.
Principle 3

Build your own platform

DreamWorks was a bid to own the studio, not just work for one.

Do this: Where you can, build or own the platform rather than only creating for others'.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Spielberg important beyond his films?

He mastered the business of storytelling as well as the craft — defining the blockbuster, taking profit participation in his hits, and co-founding DreamWorks to own a studio rather than only direct for one.

What was DreamWorks?

The major film studio Spielberg co-founded in 1994 with David Geffen and Jeffrey Katzenberg — an ambitious attempt to build a new Hollywood studio owned by its creators.
