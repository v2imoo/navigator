Morris Chang

At 55 he invented a new kind of chip company — one that builds everyone else's designs — and reshaped the tech world.

4 min read · 1980s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Morris Chang spent decades as a semiconductor executive in the United States before founding Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) in 1987 at the age of 55. His radical idea was the 'pure-play foundry': a company that manufactures chips designed by others and never competes with its own customers. This split the industry — letting 'fabless' design companies like Nvidia, Apple and Qualcomm flourish without owning fabs — and made TSMC the indispensable manufacturer at the heart of the global technology supply chain.

Chang bet that focus and trust would win: by doing only manufacturing, and brilliantly, TSMC became the partner everyone could rely on. He is a study in creating a new business model that reshapes an entire industry, in winning through focus and customer alignment, and in the strategic power of being the neutral, indispensable supplier.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Invent the model, not just the product

Chang's foundry model reshaped a whole industry.

Do this: Sometimes the biggest opportunity is a new business model that changes how the industry works, not a new product.
Principle 2

Never compete with your customers

TSMC designs nothing, so clients trust it fully.

Do this: Being the neutral, indispensable supplier — never a rival — can make you the partner everyone depends on.
Principle 3

Win through focus

By doing only manufacturing, TSMC did it better than anyone.

Do this: Deep focus on one hard thing can build an advantage that diversified competitors can't match.

In their words

In TSMC's business model, we never compete with our customers.
Without trust, there is no foundry business.
A latecomer can win by changing the rules of the game.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Morris Chang?

The founder of TSMC (1987), who pioneered the 'pure-play foundry' model — a company that only manufactures chips others design — making TSMC the indispensable manufacturer at the centre of the global tech supply chain.

What is the foundry model?

Chang's innovation of a chip company that manufactures designs for others and never competes with its customers, which enabled 'fabless' design firms like Nvidia and Apple to thrive without owning factories.
