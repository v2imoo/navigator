Falguni Nayar

She left a top banking job at 50 to start a beauty retailer — and built one of India's defining consumer brands.

4 min read · 2010s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Falguni Nayar spent nearly two decades as a successful investment banker, rising to managing director, before leaving at around 50 to found Nykaa in 2012 — an online retailer for beauty and personal-care products in a market she believed was badly underserved. She built it into one of India's leading consumer brands, blending e-commerce with physical stores and content, and took it public in a landmark 2021 IPO, becoming one of India's most prominent self-made women entrepreneurs.

Nayar combined financial discipline with a sharp read of a rising, aspirational consumer, and proved that a career switch late in life can launch a category leader. She is a study in starting boldly when conventional wisdom says it's too late, in applying hard-won skills to a new domain, and in reading an underserved market before it becomes obvious.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

It's not too late to start

Nayar launched a category leader around age 50.

Do this: Deep experience is an asset, not an expiry date; a bold start late in a career can beat an early one.
Principle 2

Carry your skills into a new domain

She applied banking discipline to consumer retail.

Do this: Hard-won skills transfer; bring your financial, operational or analytical edge into the new field you enter.
Principle 3

Read the underserved market early

She saw demand for organised beauty retail before others.

Do this: Spot the market that's large, rising and poorly served; conviction before consensus is where the opportunity is.

In their words

It's never too late to start something new.
Build a business you understand deeply as a customer.
Discipline and ambition are not opposites — you need both.
