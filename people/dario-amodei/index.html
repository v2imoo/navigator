Dario Amodei

He left the frontier of AI to build a lab around a single conviction: that safety has to come first.

4 min read · 2010s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Dario Amodei trained as a physicist and became a leading AI researcher, rising to Vice President of Research at OpenAI, where he helped drive the scaling of large language models. In 2021 he left with a group of colleagues, including his sister Daniela, to found Anthropic — a company built from the start around AI safety, with the thesis that frontier systems will be enormously powerful and must be made reliable, interpretable and steerable before, not after, they are deployed.

Amodei has become one of the most prominent voices arguing that AI progress is faster and more consequential than most realise, and that safety research must keep pace with capability. He is a study in walking away from a leading position to build around a conviction, in treating safety as the core product rather than an afterthought, and in trying to shape a technology's trajectory from inside the race.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Build around your deepest conviction

Amodei founded Anthropic on safety as the core, not a feature.

Do this: If you believe something essential is being neglected, build the organisation that puts it first.
Principle 2

Take capability seriously to take safety seriously

He argues you must understand the frontier to make it safe.

Do this: You can't responsibly shape a powerful technology from the sidelines; engage with the frontier to steer it.
Principle 3

Leave to build what you can't change inside

He left a leading role to pursue his thesis fully.

Do this: When your conviction can't be realised where you are, starting fresh may be the only way to pursue it honestly.

In their words

Powerful AI is coming, and we have to get the safety right.
The stakes are too high to treat safety as an afterthought.
I'd rather be roughly right about something important than precisely right about something trivial.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Dario Amodei?

A physicist and AI researcher, former VP of Research at OpenAI, who co-founded Anthropic in 2021 — an AI company built around the conviction that frontier systems must be made safe, interpretable and steerable.

Why did Amodei found Anthropic?

He and his co-founders believed AI safety needed to be the core of a frontier lab, not an afterthought, and left OpenAI to build a company organised around that thesis.
