Andrej Karpathy

He led self-driving AI at Tesla, helped start OpenAI — and became the field's most beloved teacher.

Part I — The story

The story

Andrej Karpathy is one of the most influential practitioners and communicators in modern AI. A founding member of OpenAI, he then led the AI team behind Tesla's Autopilot and Full Self-Driving effort, building neural networks to interpret the world from cameras at enormous scale. He coined 'Software 2.0' — the idea that neural networks learned from data are replacing hand-written code as the way we build many systems.

Beyond research, Karpathy became the field's great teacher: his deeply clear courses and tutorials on neural networks have trained a generation of practitioners, and in 2024 he founded Eureka Labs to reinvent education with AI. He is a study in combining world-class research with world-class explanation, in seeing the deeper shift a technology represents, and in the leverage of teaching — multiplying your impact by making others capable.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Pair research with teaching

Karpathy explains as brilliantly as he builds.

Do this: Teaching what you know multiplies your impact and sharpens your own understanding — don't treat it as secondary.
Principle 2

Name the deeper shift

'Software 2.0' captured how AI changes building itself.

Do this: Articulating the paradigm beneath a technology helps everyone — including you — see where it's really going.
Principle 3

Make hard things clear

His tutorials turn forbidding topics into approachable ones.

Do this: The ability to make a difficult subject genuinely clear is rare and hugely leveraged; invest in it.
