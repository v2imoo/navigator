Serena Williams

The champion whose longevity and reinvention redefined greatness — on the court and in business.

4 min read · 1995–2022

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Serena Williams is one of the greatest athletes in history, winning 23 Grand Slam singles titles across an extraordinarily long career defined by sustained dominance, resilience and repeated reinvention. Rising with her sister from Compton to the top of a sport that had rarely looked like them, she overcame injury, motherhood and constant doubt to keep winning at the highest level for over two decades.

Williams also built a business identity beyond tennis. She founded Serena Ventures, a venture-capital firm focused on backing founders overlooked by the industry — particularly women and people of colour — turning her platform and capital toward the same barriers she broke through in sport. Her career models longevity, adaptation and using a platform to open doors for others.
