John Malone

The 'cable cowboy' who mastered the art of the deal, debt, and the tax-efficient empire.

4 min read · 1973–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

John Malone built one of the great media fortunes not by creating content but by mastering the financial architecture of the cable industry — debt, deals, and tax efficiency — more deeply than anyone. As head of TCI and then Liberty Media, he assembled sprawling holdings through relentless, ingenious dealmaking that bewildered rivals and enriched shareholders.

Malone's signature insight was to run businesses for cash flow and to avoid taxes legally through clever structures — using debt to build scale, tracking stocks to isolate assets, and swaps to defer taxes. He understood that maximising after-tax value to shareholders, not reported earnings, was the real game, and he played it better than almost anyone in corporate history.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Run for cash flow, not earnings

Malone optimised after-tax cash flow, not reported profit.

Do this: Focus on the cash a business throws off, not accounting earnings.
Principle 2

Use leverage and structure

He used debt and clever structures to build and protect value.

Do this: Understand that capital structure and tax efficiency can create real value.
Principle 3

Master the deal

Malone's edge was relentless, ingenious dealmaking.

Do this: Develop deep skill in transactions; deals can build empires operations can't.
