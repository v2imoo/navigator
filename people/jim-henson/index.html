Jim Henson

The creator whose Muppets taught generations — proof that joy, craft, and heart can be a business built to last.

4 min read · 1955–1990

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Jim Henson created the Muppets and, through Sesame Street, The Muppet Show, and films, became one of the most beloved and influential entertainers of the twentieth century — using puppetry, humour, and genuine heart to delight and educate generations of children and adults alike. He elevated puppetry from a children's novelty into a sophisticated art form and built a creative company, with a troupe of brilliant collaborators, that produced a vast universe of enduring characters.

Henson combined relentless creative innovation — constantly pushing the technical craft of his art — with an unmistakable warmth and optimism that infused everything he made. He built durable intellectual property beloved across the world, and a collaborative creative culture that outlived him. He is a study in elevating a craft into an art, in building beloved and enduring IP, and in the business power of genuine joy, warmth, and creative excellence.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Elevate a craft into an art

Henson turned puppetry from novelty into a sophisticated art form.

Do this: Take a craft others dismiss and push it to the level of genuine art.
Principle 2

Build enduring, beloved IP

His characters remain loved worldwide across generations.

Do this: Create characters and worlds with enough heart to endure and compound for decades.
Principle 3

Make warmth and joy the product

Genuine optimism infused everything he made — and it sold.

Do this: Authentic joy and heart are not soft extras; they can be the core of the value.

Frequently asked questions

What did Jim Henson create?

What's the business lesson from Jim Henson?

Elevating a dismissed craft into art, building enduring beloved IP, and the real commercial power of genuine warmth, joy and creative excellence — heart as the core of lasting value.
