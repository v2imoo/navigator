Founder

Gabe Newell

The founder who built gaming's dominant platform — and a company with no bosses — by trusting talent and owning distribution.

4 min read · 1996–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Gabe Newell, a former Microsoft engineer, co-founded Valve and created landmark games before building Steam — the digital storefront that became the dominant platform for PC gaming, taking a cut of a huge share of all PC game sales worldwide. By owning distribution rather than just making games, Valve built a platform business with a powerful network effect: more players attract more developers, which attract more players, entrenching Steam as the default.

Newell also built one of the most unusual company cultures in business — Valve famously has no formal managers or hierarchy, letting employees choose which projects to work on, on the bet that talented people self-organise toward the most valuable work. Whether or not the model generalises, it is a radical experiment in trusting talent. Newell is a study in the power of owning the platform over the product, in network effects, and in culture as a deliberate, contrarian design choice.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Own the platform, not just the product

Newell built Steam to control distribution, a far bigger prize than any one game.

Do this: Owning the platform others distribute through beats owning a single product.
Principle 2

Harness the platform network effect

More players drew more developers, which drew more players.

Do this: Build the loop where each side of the platform attracts the other, and entrench it.
Principle 3

Design culture deliberately

Valve's radical no-manager structure bets on talent self-organising.

Do this: Treat culture as a deliberate design choice, even a contrarian one, not an accident.

In their words

The easiest way to stop piracy is not by putting antipiracy technology to work. It's by giving those people a service that's better than what they're receiving from the pirates.
Your most unhappy customers are your greatest source of learning.
Trust your people to find the most valuable work.
