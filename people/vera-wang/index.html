Vera Wang

The former figure skater and editor who started at 40 and made bridal wear a design category.

4 min read · 1990–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Vera Wang transformed bridal fashion — a category long treated as an afterthought — into a domain of serious design, and did it after starting her own label at the age of forty. Unable to find a wedding dress she loved for her own wedding, she spotted an underserved market and built a brand that made the bridal gown a designer object.

A competitive figure skater in her youth and then a senior fashion editor at Vogue, Wang brought a designer's eye and an editor's taste to an overlooked category, then extended her name into ready-to-wear, fragrance and lifestyle products. Her story is a powerful reminder that it is never too late to start, and that overlooked categories can be the biggest opportunities.
