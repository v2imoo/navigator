Kunal Shah

A philosophy dropout turned founder, famous as much for his sharp mental models of India as for his companies.

4 min read · 2010s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Kunal Shah built and sold Freecharge, a mobile recharge and payments company, then founded CRED, a fintech platform targeting India's most creditworthy consumers. But he is as influential for his thinking as his companies — a widely followed commentator on consumer behaviour, trust, status and the peculiarities of the Indian market, known for frameworks like 'Delta 4' (products that create a large, irreversible jump in experience) and sharp observations about why Indians behave as consumers the way they do.

Shah treats business as applied behavioural psychology and shares his mental models openly. He is a study in understanding the deep psychology of your market, in articulating frameworks that others adopt, and in building for the specific realities of a market rather than importing assumptions.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Treat business as applied psychology

Shah studies consumer behaviour deeply.

Do this: Understanding the deep psychology of your customers — status, trust, habit — often matters more than the product features.
Principle 2

Build for a 'Delta 4' jump

His framework favours irreversible leaps in experience.

Do this: Aim for products so much better that users can never go back; a large, irreversible improvement is what creates loyalty.
Principle 3

Share your frameworks openly

His mental models built his influence.

Do this: Articulating and sharing your thinking builds authority and a following that compounds beyond any single product.

In their words

Build products that give a Delta 4 improvement — users can never go back.
India is a country of many countries; design for that.
Trust is the highest-value, lowest-cost currency in business.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Kunal Shah?

An Indian entrepreneur who built and sold Freecharge and founded the fintech platform CRED, as influential for his widely followed frameworks on consumer behaviour — like 'Delta 4' — as for his companies.

What is 'Delta 4'?

Kunal Shah's framework that successful products create a large, irreversible jump in user experience — so much better that customers can never go back to the old way, which drives durable adoption.
