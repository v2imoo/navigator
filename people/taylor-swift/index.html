Taylor Swift

The artist who re-recorded her entire catalogue to reclaim it — a masterclass in owning your work.

4 min read · 2006–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Taylor Swift is one of the best-selling musical artists in history, but her most instructive achievement is a business one: when the master recordings of her early albums were sold without her, she responded by re-recording them herself — 'Taylor's Version' — creating new masters she owns outright and rendering the originals far less valuable. It was an audacious, unprecedented act of reclaiming ownership.

Swift's career is defined by control and direct connection with her audience. She has repeatedly out-manoeuvred industry structures — fighting over streaming economics, controlling her release strategy, and cultivating a fan relationship so direct it functions as its own distribution and marketing machine. Her record-shattering tours and re-recordings demonstrate the power of owning both your work and your audience relationship.
