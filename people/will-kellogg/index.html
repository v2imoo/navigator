Will Keith Kellogg

A happy accident with boiled wheat became corn flakes — and he turned breakfast into a global industry.

4 min read · 1900s–1950

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Will Keith Kellogg worked for years in the shadow of his brother at a health sanatorium, where the two accidentally discovered how to make flaked cereal from grain. Will saw the commercial potential his brother didn't, added sugar to suit public taste, and in 1906 founded what became the Kellogg Company, building corn flakes and other cereals into a staple of breakfast tables worldwide. He was a marketing pioneer, using free samples, premiums and bold advertising to create an entirely new food category.

Kellogg later gave much of his fortune to a foundation focused on children's welfare. He is a study in spotting the commercial opportunity others miss, in creating and then owning a new category, and in aggressive marketing to build a habit where none existed.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

See the opportunity others miss

Will spotted the commercial potential his brother ignored.

Do this: The person who recognises what a discovery could become commercially often matters more than who discovered it.
Principle 2

Create and own a category

He built breakfast cereal into a new staple.

Do this: Defining a new category lets you own it; be first to shape the habit and the brand that fills it.
Principle 3

Market to build the habit

He used samples and advertising to create demand.

Do this: When selling something new, invest in building the habit — sampling and vivid marketing create the demand you need.

Frequently asked questions

Who was Will Kellogg?

The founder of the Kellogg Company, who turned an accidental discovery of flaked cereal into corn flakes and built breakfast cereal into a global industry through pioneering marketing.

How did Kellogg build demand?

Through aggressive, innovative marketing — free samples, premiums and bold advertising — to create a brand-new food category and the daily habit of eating cereal for breakfast.
