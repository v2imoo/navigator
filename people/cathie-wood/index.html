Cathie Wood

She built a firm on one bet: that a handful of exponential technologies will reshape the economy.

4 min read · 2010s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Cathie Wood founded ARK Invest in 2014 on a thesis most of Wall Street found eccentric: that a small set of 'disruptive innovation' platforms — AI, robotics, genomics, energy storage, blockchain — would grow exponentially and deliver outsized returns to concentrated, high-conviction bets. She published ARK's research openly, embraced social media, and built a large following. Her flagship funds posted spectacular returns in 2020, then deep drawdowns as high-growth names fell, making her one of the most polarising figures in modern investing.

Wood is unapologetically long-horizon and thematic, sticking to her convictions through volatility that would shake most managers. She is a study in thematic conviction, in the brutal volatility that comes with betting on the future, and in the discipline — or stubbornness, depending on the year — of holding a thesis through the drawdown.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Bet on exponential change

Wood concentrates on technologies she believes will grow non-linearly.

Do this: Identify the handful of platforms that could compound exponentially, and weight toward them — but size for the volatility.
Principle 2

Publish your thinking openly

ARK shares its research and models publicly.

Do this: Open research invites scrutiny that sharpens your thesis and builds a community around it.
Principle 3

Hold through the drawdown

She stays with convictions through severe volatility.

Do this: Thematic bets on the future require stomaching large swings; decide your conviction before the storm, not during it.

In their words

Innovation is the key to growth.
We're investing in the future, and the future is uncertain and volatile.
If you're not willing to be wrong, you'll never do anything original.
