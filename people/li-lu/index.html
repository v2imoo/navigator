Li Lu

A Tiananmen student leader turned value investor — the man Charlie Munger trusted with his own money.

4 min read · 1990s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Li Lu escaped China after helping lead the 1989 Tiananmen Square protests, arrived in the United States with little, and went on to become one of the most respected value investors of his generation. He founded Himalaya Capital and applied Benjamin Graham's and Charlie Munger's principles to both American and Chinese markets — including an early, highly successful investment in BYD, the Chinese battery and electric-vehicle maker, which he brought to Munger and Berkshire's attention.

Munger entrusted Li Lu with a large portion of his personal fortune — a rare endorsement from one of history's most demanding judges of investors. Li Lu champions deep research, a long horizon, and the circle-of-competence discipline of knowing a few businesses extraordinarily well. He is a study in resilience and reinvention, in applying timeless value principles across very different markets, and in the trust earned through rigour.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Know a few businesses deeply

Li Lu concentrates on companies he understands extraordinarily well.

Do this: Depth beats breadth; master a handful of businesses within your circle of competence rather than spreading thin.
Principle 2

Apply timeless principles across markets

He used Graham and Munger's ideas in both the US and China.

Do this: Sound value principles travel; the discipline of price, value and moat works wherever you can truly understand the business.
Principle 3

Earn trust through rigour

Munger backed him because of his research depth.

Do this: Reputation and capital follow demonstrated rigour; do the work so thoroughly that the best judges want to back you.

In their words

The most important quality for an investor is temperament, not intellect.
Investing is about knowing a few things extremely well.
You make your real money through the long-term holding of great businesses.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Li Lu?

A value investor who helped lead the 1989 Tiananmen protests before fleeing to the US, founded Himalaya Capital, made a landmark early investment in BYD, and was entrusted by Charlie Munger to manage part of his personal fortune.

Why is Munger's endorsement of Li Lu significant?

Munger was one of history's most demanding judges of investors and rarely let anyone manage his money; trusting Li Lu with a large share of his own fortune was an exceptional vote of confidence.
