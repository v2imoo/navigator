Harsh Mariwala

He turned a commodity trading business into trusted consumer brands — and a model of professional, innovative Indian enterprise.

4 min read · 1970s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Harsh Mariwala transformed his family's commodity trading business into Marico, one of India's leading consumer-goods companies, building trusted brands like Parachute coconut oil and Saffola. His insight was to move from selling undifferentiated commodities to building branded, value-added consumer products — and to professionalise the family business with modern management, innovation and a strong, empowering culture. He pioneered consumer packaging and distribution innovations in the Indian market.

Mariwala also champions innovation and entrepreneurship through the Marico Innovation Foundation and Ascent Foundation. He is a study in moving up the value chain from commodity to brand, in professionalising a family enterprise, and in building an innovative, people-first company culture.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Move from commodity to brand

Mariwala turned undifferentiated oil into trusted brands.

Do this: Escape the commodity trap by building branded, value-added products; branding captures margin that trading never can.
Principle 2

Professionalise the family business

He brought modern management to a family firm.

Do this: Family enterprises scale by adopting professional management, systems and culture rather than staying personal and ad hoc.
Principle 3

Build an empowering culture

His people-first culture drove Marico's success.

Do this: A strong, empowering culture that trusts and develops people is a durable competitive advantage.

In their words

Move up the value chain, always.
Trust your people and give them freedom to perform.
Innovation is not a department; it is a mindset.
