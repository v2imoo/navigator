Simone Biles

She pushed her sport past its known limits — then showed the world that protecting your mind is strength, not weakness.

4 min read · 2010s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Simone Biles is the most decorated gymnast in history, with more world and Olympic medals than anyone, and has pushed the sport to new frontiers — performing skills so difficult that several are named after her because no one else had landed them in competition. She overcame a difficult childhood in foster care to redefine what was physically possible in gymnastics.

At the 2020 Olympics she withdrew from events to protect her mental health, experiencing the dangerous disorientation gymnasts call the 'twisties', and in doing so reshaped the global conversation about athletes' wellbeing — demonstrating that knowing when to step back can be its own form of courage and strength. She is a study in pushing the limits of a discipline, in the primacy of mental as well as physical health, and in redefining strength to include self-protection.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Push past the known limits

Biles performs skills no one had landed before.

Do this: Don't accept the current ceiling of your field as fixed; the frontier moves for those willing to attempt it.
Principle 2

Protect the mind as fiercely as the body

She stepped back to safeguard her mental health.

Do this: Peak performance depends on mental health; knowing when to pause is strength, not weakness.
Principle 3

Redefine what strength means

Her withdrawal reframed courage for a generation.

Do this: Sometimes the bravest, strongest act is to say 'not now' — self-protection can be leadership.

In their words

I'd rather regret the risks that didn't work out than the chances I didn't take.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Simone Biles considered the greatest gymnast?

She holds more world and Olympic medals than any gymnast in history and has performed skills so difficult that several are officially named after her, pushing the sport's limits further than anyone.

Why was Biles's 2020 withdrawal significant?

By stepping back from events to protect her mental health amid the dangerous disorientation called the 'twisties', she reshaped the global conversation on athlete wellbeing and redefined strength to include self-protection.
