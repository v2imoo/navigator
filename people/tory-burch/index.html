Tory Burch

The founder who built a global 'accessible luxury' brand by filling the gap between mass and designer.

4 min read · 2004–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Tory Burch built a global fashion brand by identifying an underserved space in the market: stylish, well-made clothing and accessories priced between fast fashion and high-end designer labels — 'accessible luxury' for women who wanted quality and design without couture prices. Her signature ballet flats and the distinctive medallion logo became instantly recognisable.

Launching in 2004 with a clear aesthetic and a strong retail presence, Burch grew the brand rapidly, helped by an early boost when Oprah Winfrey championed it. She combined an appealing product with savvy branding and, later, a foundation supporting women entrepreneurs — building both a business and a mission around female ambition.

Burch's success is a case study in positioning — finding the white space between market segments — and in building a recognisable brand identity that lets a company command a premium without competing at the very top of the market.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Find the white space

Burch targeted the gap between fast fashion and designer luxury.

Do this: Look for an underserved space between existing market segments.
Principle 2

Build a recognisable identity

A signature product and logo made the brand instantly known.

Do this: Create distinctive, recognisable signatures that make your brand memorable.
Principle 3

Tie the brand to a mission

Her foundation for women entrepreneurs reinforced the brand's meaning.

Do this: Connect your brand to a mission that deepens customers' affinity.

Frequently asked questions

What is 'accessible luxury'?

The market space Tory Burch built her brand in — stylish, well-made products priced between fast fashion and high-end designer labels, offering design and quality without couture prices.

How did Tory Burch's brand grow so fast?

Through a clear aesthetic, a recognisable signature product and logo, strong retail presence, and an early boost from Oprah Winfrey's endorsement.
