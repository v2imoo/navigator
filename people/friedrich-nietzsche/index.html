Friedrich Nietzsche

The philosopher who challenged us to create our own values and become who we are.

4 min read · 1872–1889

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Friedrich Nietzsche was one of the most provocative and influential philosophers of the modern age, challenging the foundations of morality, religion and truth with a radical demand: that individuals must create their own values and meaning rather than inherit them passively. His ideas — the 'will to power', the übermensch, 'that which does not kill me makes me stronger' — are among the most quoted and most misunderstood in philosophy.

At the core of Nietzsche's thought is self-overcoming: the idea that a meaningful life requires continually surpassing yourself, embracing struggle and even suffering as the raw material of growth, and having the courage to affirm your own existence and forge your own path. Though his work is complex and often bleak, its call to take radical ownership of your values, purpose and becoming resonates powerfully with anyone building an unconventional life or venture.

Nietzsche challenges us to take radical ownership of our values and becoming — to create rather than inherit meaning, to embrace struggle as the material of growth, and to have the courage to forge our own path. However one reads him, that demand for self-authorship is bracing for any independent thinker or builder.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Create your own values

Nietzsche demanded we forge meaning rather than inherit it.

Do this: Question inherited values and consciously choose the ones you'll live by.
Principle 2

Embrace struggle as growth

He saw hardship as the raw material of self-overcoming.

Do this: Treat struggle and adversity as the material from which you grow stronger.
Principle 3

Continually overcome yourself

His ideal was endless self-surpassing.

Do this: Make surpassing your former self a continual practice, not a one-time goal.

In their words

That which does not kill us makes us stronger.
He who has a why to live can bear almost any how.
Become who you are.

Frequently asked questions

What is Nietzsche's core idea?

That individuals must create their own values and meaning rather than passively inherit them — embracing struggle and self-overcoming, and having the courage to affirm their existence and forge their own path.

What does 'that which does not kill me makes me stronger' mean?

Nietzsche's idea that adversity and struggle, when survived, become the raw material of growth and self-overcoming — though his philosophy is far richer and more complex than the popular slogan.
