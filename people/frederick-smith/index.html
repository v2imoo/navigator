Frederick W. Smith

The founder who invented overnight delivery — and the hub-and-spoke network that made it possible.

4 min read · 1971–2022

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Frederick W. Smith founded FedEx and, in doing so, created an entire industry: guaranteed overnight delivery. The core idea, which he reportedly sketched as a university student, was counter-intuitive — route every package, no matter its origin or destination, through a single central hub overnight, then fly it out to its destination. The 'hub-and-spoke' system made nationwide next-day delivery economically possible for the first time.

A decorated Marine who served in Vietnam, Smith launched Federal Express in 1971 and nearly went bankrupt in its early years before the model proved itself. FedEx grew into a global logistics giant handling millions of shipments a day and became indispensable to modern commerce and, later, e-commerce.

Stepped down as CEO

Smith stepped down as CEO in 2022 and died in 2025. His hub-and-spoke insight — that centralising everything through one point could be more efficient than direct routing — became a foundational concept in logistics far beyond FedEx.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Rethink the network, not the vehicle

Smith's breakthrough was routing everything through a central hub.

Do this: Look for a structural redesign of the system, not just a faster version of the old one.
Principle 2

Create the category

FedEx didn't compete in overnight delivery; it invented it.

Do this: Sometimes the biggest opportunity is a service that doesn't yet exist.
Principle 3

Survive the early valley

FedEx nearly failed before the model proved out.

Do this: Expect a hard early stretch before a novel model reaches viable scale.

In their words

Frequently asked questions

What did Fred Smith invent?

Guaranteed overnight delivery, enabled by the 'hub-and-spoke' network — routing every package through a central hub overnight before flying it out — which created the modern express-logistics industry.

What is the hub-and-spoke system?

A network design where all shipments route through one central hub rather than point-to-point, which made nationwide next-day delivery economically viable for the first time.
