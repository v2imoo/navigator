Verghese Kurien

He turned millions of small dairy farmers into owners of a national brand — and made India the world's biggest milk producer.

4 min read · 1950s–2000s

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Verghese Kurien built the cooperative model that transformed India from a milk-deficient country into the largest milk producer in the world. Starting in Anand, Gujarat, he helped create Amul — a dairy owned by the farmers themselves — and then scaled the model nationally through 'Operation Flood', linking millions of small producers directly to urban markets and cutting out exploitative middlemen. Farmers kept the value, and the cooperative became a household brand.

Kurien's genius was organisational and institutional: he designed a structure in which ordinary villagers owned and governed a modern, professionally managed enterprise. He is a study in cooperative ownership as a tool for mass prosperity, in cutting out the intermediaries who capture value, and in building a durable institution that puts economic power in the hands of the many.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Give ownership to the producers

Kurien's cooperatives were owned by the farmers themselves.

Do this: Structure ventures so those doing the work own the value; ownership aligns incentives and spreads prosperity.
Principle 2

Cut out the value-capturing middleman

He linked farmers directly to markets.

Do this: Find where intermediaries extract value without adding it, and design them out so producers keep the gains.
Principle 3

Professionalise without disempowering

Villagers owned it; professionals ran it well.

Do this: Combine grassroots ownership with professional management so the enterprise is both fair and competitive.

Frequently asked questions

Who was Verghese Kurien?

The 'Father of India's White Revolution' — he built Amul and led Operation Flood, a cooperative dairy model owned by farmers that made India the world's largest milk producer and lifted millions of rural livelihoods.

What was Operation Flood?

Kurien's national programme that scaled the farmer-owned dairy cooperative model across India, linking millions of small milk producers directly to urban markets and transforming the country's dairy economy.
