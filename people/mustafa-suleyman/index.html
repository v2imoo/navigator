Mustafa Suleyman

He co-built DeepMind, wrote the warning label for the AI age, and now runs Microsoft's AI.

4 min read · 2010s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Mustafa Suleyman co-founded DeepMind in 2010 alongside Demis Hassabis, leading its applied and ethics work before the Google acquisition. He later co-founded Inflection AI, building a personal-assistant chatbot, and in 2024 joined Microsoft as CEO of its consumer AI division, Microsoft AI. His 2023 book The Coming Wave became an influential argument that the twin waves of AI and synthetic biology will be extraordinarily powerful and extraordinarily hard to contain — and that 'containment' must become society's central technical and political challenge.

Suleyman sits at an unusual intersection of building frontier AI and publicly warning about its risks. He is a study in co-founding category-defining organisations, in thinking seriously about the governance of a technology you're helping build, and in moving between research, entrepreneurship and big-company leadership while carrying a consistent thesis.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Think about containment, not just capability

Suleyman argues powerful tech must be governable.

Do this: When building something powerful, invest as much thought in how to contain and govern it as in making it work.
Principle 2

Carry one thesis across roles

His view on AI's stakes stayed consistent from DeepMind to Microsoft.

Do this: A clear, consistent thesis lets you move between research, startups and big companies without losing direction.
Principle 3

Warn from the inside

He publicly flags risks of the technology he builds.

Do this: Being a credible critic of a field you help lead carries weight that pure outsiders can't match — use it responsibly.

In their words

The coming wave is defined by two core technologies: AI and synthetic biology.
Containment is not optional.
The best way to understand a technology is to try to build it.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Mustafa Suleyman?

A co-founder of DeepMind (2010) who later co-founded Inflection AI and became CEO of Microsoft AI in 2024; his book The Coming Wave argues AI and synthetic biology must be made containable.

What is 'The Coming Wave' about?

Suleyman's argument that AI and synthetic biology are about to become extraordinarily powerful and hard to contain, making 'containment' — governing and limiting such technologies — the central challenge of the age.
