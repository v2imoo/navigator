Nandan Nilekani

He helped build an IT giant, then gave a billion people a digital identity — public infrastructure at national scale.

4 min read · 1980s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Nandan Nilekani co-founded Infosys in 1981, helping build it into one of India's defining technology companies and a symbol of the country's software industry. He then did something unusual for a successful entrepreneur: he entered government to lead the creation of Aadhaar, a biometric digital identity system that has enrolled over a billion people, becoming the backbone for direct benefit transfers, digital payments and services at a scale no country had attempted.

Nilekani has championed 'digital public infrastructure' — open, foundational systems like identity and payments that the private sector can build on — as a model for development. He is a study in moving from private success to public mission, in building platforms that others create value on top of, and in designing foundational systems for a billion-person scale.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Build the platform others build on

Aadhaar is foundational infrastructure, not an end product.

Do this: Create the open layer that many others can build on top of; a platform's value compounds far beyond any single app.
Principle 2

Move from private success to public mission

Nilekani took his skills into government service.

Do this: Hard-won private-sector capability can have its largest impact aimed at public problems at national scale.
Principle 3

Design for a billion from the start

Aadhaar was architected for unprecedented scale.

Do this: When the goal is population-scale, bake scale and simplicity into the foundation; you can't retrofit it later.

In their words

Imagining India means imagining its systems.
Digital public infrastructure is the great equaliser.
Build foundations, and let a thousand applications bloom.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Nandan Nilekani?

A co-founder of Infosys who later led the creation of Aadhaar, India's biometric digital identity system enrolling over a billion people, and a leading champion of 'digital public infrastructure'.

What is Aadhaar?

A national digital identity system Nilekani architected, giving over a billion Indians a verifiable identity that underpins direct benefit transfers, digital payments and public services at unprecedented scale.
