Jensen Huang

The founder who spent two decades building the tools of the AI age before the age arrived.

4 min read · 1993–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Jensen Huang co-founded NVIDIA in 1993 and has led it ever since — an extraordinary tenure that spanned the company's transformation from a maker of gaming graphics chips into the essential supplier of the artificial-intelligence era. His defining bet was investing, for a decade before it paid off, in CUDA software that turned NVIDIA's chips into general engines for parallel computation.

When deep learning exploded, the entire field already ran on NVIDIA hardware because it ran on CUDA — a moat Huang had quietly dug through years the market saw no reason for. In 2025 NVIDIA became the first company ever to reach a five-trillion-dollar valuation.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Invest before the payoff

Huang funded CUDA for a decade before AI made it indispensable — the deepest moats are dug early.

Do this: Build the capability that will matter later, before the market rewards it.
Principle 2

Own the software, not just the chip

CUDA, not the silicon, locked in the AI field; wrap a commodity in an ecosystem.

Do this: Wrap your core product in tools people build their work on.
Principle 3

Ride network effects

Every developer who learned CUDA made NVIDIA the default for the next.

Do this: Design so each new user makes your product the safer choice for the following one.

Frequently asked questions

Why did NVIDIA win the AI era?

Because Huang invested for years in CUDA, the software the AI field learned to build on, so when deep learning arrived it already required NVIDIA hardware.

How long has Jensen Huang led NVIDIA?

He co-founded it in 1993 and has been CEO throughout — more than three decades.
