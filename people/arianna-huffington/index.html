Arianna Huffington

The founder who built a media giant — then, after collapsing from exhaustion, built a company to fight burnout.

4 min read · 2005–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Arianna Huffington co-founded The Huffington Post in 2005, building it into one of the most influential news-and-commentary sites of the internet era — blending professional journalism, a vast network of free contributors, and a sharp understanding of the emerging online-media landscape — and sold it to AOL for over $300 million. A Greek immigrant and author, she had reinvented herself repeatedly before becoming a media mogul.

Then, at the height of her success, she collapsed from exhaustion and sleep deprivation, breaking her cheekbone — a wake-up call that led to her next act. In 2016 she left HuffPost to found Thrive Global, a company dedicated to combating the burnout and stress epidemic she had personified, championing sleep, wellbeing, and sustainable performance. She is a study in reinvention across careers, in reading and riding a media shift, and in turning a personal crisis into a new mission and business.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Reinvent across eras

Huffington remade herself repeatedly — author, then media mogul, then wellbeing founder.

Do this: Be willing to reinvent yourself across careers as the world and you change.
Principle 2

Read and ride the platform shift

She understood the emerging online-media landscape early.

Do this: Spot where the media or platform landscape is shifting and build for it early.
Principle 3

Turn personal crisis into mission

Her burnout collapse became the seed of Thrive Global.

Do this: A personal crisis, faced honestly, can become your next mission and business.

In their words

We think, mistakenly, that success is the result of the amount of time we put in, rather than the quality of the time.
Burnout is not the price you have to pay for success.
Failure is not the opposite of success; it's part of success.
