Tobias Lütke

He set out to sell snowboards online, hated the software, built his own — and armed a generation of entrepreneurs.

4 min read · 2000s–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Tobias Lütke, a German-born programmer, moved to Canada and in 2004 tried to open an online snowboard store called Snowdevil. Finding the available e-commerce software terrible, he built his own platform instead — and realised the platform was the real business. That became Shopify, launched in 2006, which grew into the dominant software for letting anyone set up and run an online store, powering millions of businesses and tens of billions in sales.

Lütke is known for a thoughtful, first-principles leadership style — treating the company as a system to be continually redesigned, writing about management as craft, and championing the independent merchant against the dominance of giant marketplaces. He is a study in the classic pattern of building the tool you needed, in choosing to arm others rather than compete with them, and in treating the company itself as a product to engineer.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Build the tool you needed

Lütke made Shopify because the existing software failed him.

Do this: If you can't find a good tool for your own problem, building it may be a bigger business than the original idea.
Principle 2

Arm others instead of competing

Shopify empowers independent merchants rather than replacing them.

Do this: Enabling many others to succeed can be a larger, more durable business than trying to win the market yourself.
Principle 3

Engineer the company itself

He treats the organisation as a system to redesign continually.

Do this: Apply the same first-principles rigour to how your company works as you do to your product.

In their words

Entrepreneurship is the act of taking personal responsibility for improving the world.
Shopify arms the rebels.
The company is the ultimate product.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Tobias Lütke?

The German-born programmer who co-founded Shopify in 2006 after building his own e-commerce software for a snowboard store, growing it into the dominant platform for independent online merchants.

What is Shopify's core idea?

To 'arm the rebels' — give any individual or small business the software to set up and run their own online store, empowering independent merchants rather than funnelling them into giant marketplaces.
