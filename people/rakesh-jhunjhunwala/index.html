Rakesh Jhunjhunwala

The 'Big Bull' turned a tiny stake into a fortune by betting, for decades, on the India growth story.

4 min read · 1980s–2022

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Rakesh Jhunjhunwala was India's best-known and most celebrated stock-market investor, nicknamed the 'Big Bull'. Starting with a tiny sum as a young chartered accountant in the 1980s, he built an enormous fortune through long-term equity investing, famously making concentrated, high-conviction bets — above all on the Indian growth story, which he championed relentlessly as a believer in the country's rise. His investment in Titan became one of the most storied trades in Indian market history.

Part Graham-and-Buffett value investor, part bold trader, he combined deep conviction with an irrepressible optimism about India. He is a study in long-term conviction investing, in backing a structural growth story early and holding it, and in the discipline to concentrate on your strongest ideas.

Part II — The playbook

The playbook

Principle 1

Bet the structural story early

Jhunjhunwala backed India's rise and held for decades.

Do this: Identify the big structural growth story, invest in it early, and hold through the volatility; conviction over decades compounds.
Principle 2

Concentrate on your best ideas

He made bold, concentrated bets.

Do this: Rather than spreading thin, put real weight behind your highest-conviction ideas; concentration rewards being right.
Principle 3

Pair conviction with optimism

His bullishness on India was relentless.

Do this: A reasoned, durable optimism about a trend can be an edge; believe in the story longer than the sceptics.
