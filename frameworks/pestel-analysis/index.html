PESTEL Analysis

Scan the big external forces — Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal.

Contents

Overview

PESTEL is a checklist for scanning the large external forces that shape a business but sit outside its control: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental and Legal factors. It's a deliberately broad sweep, meant to catch the macro shifts that a narrower competitive analysis would miss.

It pairs naturally with SWOT — PESTEL surfaces the external opportunities and threats — and with Five Forces, giving the wide-angle context around the industry-level view.

When to use it

Understanding the macro-environment before entering a market, planning long-term, or assessing external risk.

How to use it

Step 1

Political

Government policy, stability, trade, tax — how politics could help or hurt you.

Step 2

Economic

Growth, inflation, rates, employment, exchange rates shaping demand and cost.

Step 3

Social

Demographics, culture, attitudes and lifestyle trends affecting what people want.

Step 4

Technological

Innovation, automation and shifts that could disrupt or enable your model.

Step 5

Environmental

Climate, sustainability pressures, resource constraints and expectations.

Step 6

Legal

Regulation, employment and consumer law, IP — the rules you must operate within.

Worked example

A company weighing expansion into a new country runs PESTEL and catches, under Legal and Political, an incoming data-localisation law and unstable trade relations that a product-focused analysis missed entirely — factors serious enough to delay entry. The wide scan surfaced a deal-breaker hiding outside the industry.

Common pitfalls

  • Generating a huge list with no judgement about which factors actually matter.
  • A one-time snapshot of forces that are constantly changing.
  • Stopping at description instead of drawing implications for strategy.

Frequently asked questions

What does PESTEL stand for?

Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental and Legal — the six categories of external macro forces to scan.

How does PESTEL relate to SWOT?

PESTEL surfaces the external forces that become the Opportunities and Threats in a SWOT; it's the wide macro scan feeding the internal-vs-external picture.
