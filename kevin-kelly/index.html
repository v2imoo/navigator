Kevin Kelly

The technology sage whose ideas — like '1,000 True Fans' — quietly shaped the creator economy.

4 min read · 1984–present

Contents

Part I — The story

The story

Kevin Kelly, a co-founder of Wired magazine, is one of the most influential technology thinkers of the internet age — a wide-ranging forecaster whose essays have quietly shaped how founders and creators understand the world. His 2008 essay '1,000 True Fans' argued that a creator needs only a thousand dedicated supporters, each paying a modest amount, to make a living — an idea that became a founding principle of the creator economy.

Kelly writes about the long-term direction of technology with unusual optimism and breadth, from the 'inevitable' forces shaping the future to practical wisdom about tools and living. He thinks in decades, looks for the deep patterns beneath the churn of news, and distils them into memorable, actionable ideas that entrepreneurs return to again and again.
