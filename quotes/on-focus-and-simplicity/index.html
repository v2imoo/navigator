Focus

On Focus & Simplicity

The discipline of doing less, better — in the words of people who lived it.

Contents

Overview

Great work is usually the result of subtraction, not addition. These lines capture the discipline of focus, and each is followed by a note on how to apply it.

The quotes

Stay hungry. Stay foolish.— Steve Jobs

A charge to keep the curiosity and willingness to look naive that fade with success — the traits that let you keep learning and taking risks.

Fashion changes, but style endures.— Coco Chanel

Chase the durable thing, not the trend. Style — a consistent point of view — outlasts whatever is fashionable this season.

The secret of success is to do the common things uncommonly well.— John D. Rockefeller

Excellence is rarely exotic. It usually means executing ordinary tasks to a standard almost no one else bothers with.

Control your expenses better than your competition.— Sam Walton

Walton's whole strategy in one line: a durable advantage can be built from unglamorous discipline that rivals won't match.
