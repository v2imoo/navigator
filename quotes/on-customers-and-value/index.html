On Customers & Value

The businesses that last are obsessed with the people they serve.

Contents

Overview

Every durable company, however different, shares an obsession with delivering value to the customer. These lines make the point.

The quotes

There is only one boss — the customer.— Sam Walton

The customer can fire everyone by spending elsewhere. Keeping that fact central is what keeps a business honest and alive.

People don't know what they want until you show it to them.— Steve Jobs

Serving customers isn't only asking them; sometimes it's understanding a need they can't yet articulate and building the answer.

In order to be irreplaceable one must always be different.— Coco Chanel

Value, for a brand, comes from distinctiveness. Being the same as everyone else is the surest route to being replaceable.
