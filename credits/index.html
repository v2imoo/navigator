Credits &amp; Sources

Contents

Navigator is written from a wide range of public sources: biographies, company filings, reputable journalism, and reference works. Each profile’s What to read next section points to the primary books behind it.

Images

Portraits and logos are fetched at page-load from Wikipedia via the MediaWiki API, and remain the property of their respective rights-holders. Where an image can’t be loaded, a simple monogram is shown instead.

Approach

All prose is original and written for Navigator. Facts are drawn from sources; wording is not copied. Where accounts differ, the aim is a fair, accurate summary.
