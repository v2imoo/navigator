Cornered Resource

When you alone control something valuable — a patent, a talent, a deposit, a deal — rivals simply can't have it.

Contents

Overview

A cornered-resource moat exists when a company has preferential access to a coveted asset that others cannot obtain on equal terms: a key patent, an exclusive licence, a unique location or mineral deposit, or a singular person or team whose output can't be replicated.

The power is exclusivity. As long as the resource is genuinely scarce, controlled, and valuable, competitors are locked out of the advantage it confers, regardless of how well they execute elsewhere.

How it works

Step 1

The company secures exclusive or preferential control of a scarce, valuable input.

Step 2

That control cannot be bypassed — rivals can't buy, build, or copy the resource on equal terms.

Step 3

The advantage persists as long as the resource stays scarce and the control stays secure.

Where you see it

Pharma patents

An exclusive patent locks rivals out of a drug for the life of the protection.

Pixar's talent

A singular creative team produced hit after hit that competitors couldn't replicate.

How to build it

  • Identify and lock up the scarce input your business depends on — patents, licences, key hires, supply.
  • Structure exclusivity so it can't be easily bypassed or expire quietly.
  • Keep the resource genuinely differentiated — a 'cornered' commodity is no moat.

Pitfalls

  • Patents and licences expire; talent can leave; deposits deplete — the moat has a clock.
  • Over-reliance on one person or asset is fragile if it walks out the door.
  • A resource only counts if it's genuinely scarce and valuable, not merely owned.

Frequently asked questions

Can a person be a cornered resource?

Yes — a singular talent or team whose output rivals can't replicate can be a moat, though it's fragile if that person can leave.

What's the main weakness of this moat?

Impermanence. Patents expire, licences lapse, talent departs and deposits run out — so the advantage often has a built-in clock.
