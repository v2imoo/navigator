Moats

The 7 Moats

The seven sources of durable competitive advantage — the reasons a great business stays great. Each one explained with how it works, where you see it, how to build it, and how it fails.

Power 1 of 7
Scale Economies

When bigger means cheaper per unit, the largest player can price below everyone and stay profitable.

Power 2 of 7
Network Economies

When each new user makes the product more valuable to every other user, the biggest network wins by default.

Power 3 of 7
Switching Costs

When leaving costs the customer money, time, or risk, they stay — even for a better alternative.

Power 4 of 7
Branding

When a name itself makes people pay more or feel more certain, the brand is the moat.

Power 5 of 7
Cornered Resource

When you alone control something valuable — a patent, a talent, a deposit, a deal — rivals simply can't have it.

Power 6 of 7
Process Power

When your way of working is better and can't be copied quickly, the process itself is the moat.

Power 7 of 7
Counter-Positioning

When a newcomer adopts a model the incumbent can't copy without damaging its own business, the incumbent is trapped.
