Book Summaries

The essential books behind Navigator, distilled to their big ideas and key lessons — and linked to the people and models they illuminate. New summaries publish regularly.

Chip War

Chris Miller — How semiconductors became the most critical technology on earth — and the fight to control them.

Good to Great

Jim Collins — What separates companies that make the leap to greatness from those that stay merely good.

The Everything Store

Titan

Zero to One

Peter Thiel with Blake Masters — Build something genuinely new — and aim for monopoly, not competition.
